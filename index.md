<h2 class="project-name">No More HDR</h2>

<p class="project-description">Modern phones tuck extra brightness data into photos, so they flash eye-searingly bright the moment you open or forward them. No More HDR finds those photos and makes calm, normal-looking copies, processed entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded.</p>
