<h2 class="project-name">No More HDR</h2>

<p class="project-description">Some photos look normal in your library but glow too bright when you send or post them. That is hidden HDR: extra brightness stored inside the photo. No More HDR finds those photos and turns them into normal SDR copies.</p>
